Privacy Policy for QR Studio

Last Updated: October 9, 2026
Application ID: com.rafiqdev.qrstudio
Developer: Muhammad Rafiq

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

QR Studio is built with privacy-by-design. All QR code generation, customization, payload rendering, and code scanning operate 100% locally and privately on your physical device.

We do not operate remote databases, servers, or cloud repositories that collect, store, or transmit your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

None. We do not collect, process, sell, or request any personal identifying information (PII) such as your name, phone number, email address, physical address, or location.

B. Generated Data & Payloads

Any data entered into the generator (such as URLs, Wi-Fi keys, contact vCards, or text) is processed in-memory and saved exclusively in local sandboxed storage on your device. It never leaves your phone.

C. Camera Access

Camera access is requested solely to scan optical QR codes in real time. The feed is processed on-device; no images or video recordings are saved or uploaded.

3. Permissions Requested

Permission Usage Purpose
android.permission.CAMERA Used exclusively for live camera scanning of optical QR codes.
android.permission.VIBRATE Used to trigger subtle tactile feedback on scan success and button taps.
android.permission.INTERNET Standard network capabilities for opening web links in your browser.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

  • No Analytics Trackers: We do not embed behavioral tracking SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, or Adjust).
  • No Advertising: The app does not display third-party advertisements or track advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control. Clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently purges all data immediately.

6. Children's Privacy

QR Studio does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children under 13. The app is safe for all audiences and complies with Google Play Families Policy and COPPA.
